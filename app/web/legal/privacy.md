## The short version

- **Your contract is not stored.** It is written to temporary storage, scanned,
  and deleted when the scan finishes. We keep no copy and no report.
- **No account is needed**, so we hold no profile about you.
- **The AI gets one sentence at a time**, not your document: only the clause that
  the rule engine already flagged, plus the rule's own description. You can turn
  the AI off entirely before scanning.
- **No advertising, no tracking pixels, no analytics cookies.**
- **We never use your documents to train a model.**

## 1. What we collect

**The document you upload.** The file, its name, and the text extracted from it.
Held in memory and in a temporary folder for the duration of the scan only.

**Technical information** needed to serve a web page and keep it working: IP
address, browser type and version, the pages requested, timestamps, and error
information. This exists in server and hosting logs.

**Your email address**, if you write to us for support, or if you ask to be told
about the launch. We keep that correspondence to answer you.

**Payment information**, when you buy a report: the card is handled entirely by
Stripe on their checkout page. What reaches us is a Stripe transaction reference,
the amount paid and whether the payment succeeded — we read that reference once,
to decide whether to unlock the report. **We never see or store your card number**,
and we do not keep a copy of the transaction beyond the confirmation the report
needs.

## 2. What we do not collect

We do not require an account, so we do not hold a password, a profile or a
purchase history keyed to you. We do not store your reports. We do not buy
contact lists, and we do not run advertising or cross-site tracking on this site.

## 3. Why we process it, and on what legal basis

| Purpose | Data | Legal basis (where GDPR applies) |
|---|---|---|
| Produce your report | Uploaded document | Performance of a contract with you |
| Keep the service secure and working | Technical logs | Legitimate interests (security, debugging) |
| Answer your support request | Email, correspondence | Legitimate interests, or consent where required |
| Take and account for payment | Stripe transaction data | Performance of a contract; legal obligation (tax) |

We do not use your document for any purpose other than producing your report.

## 4. Who else sees it

We use a small number of processors. Each is bound to use the data only to
provide its service to us.

- **Hosting provider** — runs the application and holds the temporary file while
  it is being scanned.
- **AI model provider (DeepSeek)** — receives the individual flagged sentences
  and the rule text, to write the plain-English explanation. It does not receive
  your full document, your name or your email.
- **Stripe** — payment processing when you buy a report. Stripe is an
  independent controller for its own fraud-prevention and regulatory purposes;
  see [Stripe's privacy policy](https://stripe.com/privacy).
- **Email provider** — delivers and stores support correspondence.

We do not sell your data, and we do not share it for advertising. We may disclose
information if the law requires it, or to establish or defend a legal claim.

## 5. International transfers

We operate from {{governing_law}}, and our processors are located in various
countries, including the United States and, for the AI provider, China. Your
document may therefore be processed outside the country you live in.

Where personal data leaves the EEA, the UK or Switzerland, we rely on appropriate
safeguards (standard contractual clauses or an equivalent mechanism). If you need
to know which mechanism applies to a particular transfer, ask us at {{email}}.

**If this matters to you, turn the AI explanations off before scanning.** The
rule engine runs on our own infrastructure and the scan is complete without the
AI step; the flagged clauses are the same either way.

## 6. How long we keep it

- **Uploaded document:** deleted when the scan ends — seconds, not days. No copy
  is retained.
- **Generated report:** not stored by us. It lives in your browser session and in
  the file you download.
- **Server logs:** up to 30 days, then deleted or aggregated.
- **Support email:** up to 24 months after the conversation ends.
- **Payment records:** held by Stripe, not by us. We unlock a report from the
  transaction reference and keep no separate purchase history keyed to you.

## 7. Your rights

Depending on where you live — the EEA, the UK, Switzerland, California and several
other US states, and elsewhere — you may have the right to:

- **know** whether we hold personal data about you, and get a copy;
- **correct** it if it is wrong;

- **object to** or **restrict** certain processing;
- **port** data you gave us to another provider;
- **withdraw consent** at any time, where processing is based on consent;
- **not be discriminated against** for exercising these rights.

We do not sell or share personal data as those terms are defined by the CCPA/CPRA,
so there is nothing to opt out of.

To exercise a right, email {{email}}. We answer within 30 days, and we may need to
verify that the request is yours. If we refuse, we will tell you why and how to

If you are in the EEA or the UK you also have the right to complain to your

## 8. Cookies and local storage

The site sets no advertising or analytics cookies. Streamlit, the framework the
interface is built on, keeps a session token so that the page you are looking at
stays connected to the server that is running your scan; it disappears when you
close the tab. Nothing is written to your device that identifies you across
sites.

## 9. Security

Documents are transferred over HTTPS (once the site is served over HTTPS, which is
required in production), held only for the length of the scan, and deleted
afterwards. Access to the servers is restricted and logged.

No system is perfectly secure. If a breach affects your personal data, we will
notify you and the relevant authority where the law requires it.

## 10. Children

The Service is for people aged 18 and over. We do not knowingly collect data from
children. If you believe a child has used the Service, write to {{email}} and we
will delete the data.

## 11. Automated processing

The scan is automated, and it produces a report rather than a decision about you:
we do not make automated decisions that produce legal or similarly significant
effects about any person. The AI explanations are generated text about a clause,
not an assessment of you.
