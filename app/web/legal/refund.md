# Refund Policy

**Effective date:** {{effective}}

This policy explains when {{operator}} refunds money for {{product}}. It forms
part of our [Terms of Service](terms).

## The short version

- **{{price}} per contract report, charged once.** No subscription, no account,
  no recurring charge. Nothing is charged until you confirm it on Stripe's
  checkout page.
- **If the scan failed** — a file error, an outage, a report that never rendered —
  we refund in full, on request, without argument.
- **A report you received is not refundable** because you disagree with its
  findings. The report is a screening aid, not a guarantee, and "it flagged a
  clause I think is fine" is a normal result — see section 4.
- Ask within **7 days** of the charge, at {{email}}, and we will answer within
  **5 business days**.

## 1. What you pay for

One payment of {{price}} buys one complete report for one contract: every flagged
clause, the sentence quoted from your document, why it matters, and the wording to
send back to your client.

You can scan a contract and read a free preview — the single most serious problem
we found — before paying anything. The preview is a real result, not a blank
form: you see the finding, the quote and the explanation for that one clause.

## 2. How payment works

Payments are processed by Stripe on their checkout page. We do not receive, see or
store your card number. When the payment is confirmed, Stripe sends you back to the
report, and recording that confirmation is what unlocks it.

A payment covers the contract you scanned. Uploading a different contract requires
a second payment. Re-opening the report you already paid for does not.

## 3. When we refund in full

We refund the charge, without argument, if:

- **the scan failed** — a file error, an outage, or a report that never rendered,
  and a retry did not fix it;
- **you paid but the full report never unlocked**, and we could not unlock it for
  you;
- **you were charged twice** for the same scan, or charged after cancelling;
- **you were charged an amount you did not agree to** at checkout;
- **a charge was not authorised by you**, in which case tell us immediately and
  also tell your bank.

## 4. When we do not refund

- **You received a complete report and disagree with it.** The report lists
  clauses that match a fixed library of red-flag patterns, with the sentence
  quoted so you can judge it yourself. It is not a legal opinion, and it does not
  promise that a flagged clause is unenforceable — or that an unflagged contract
  is safe. Disagreement with a finding is not a defect.
- **You uploaded the wrong file, or a file outside the supported formats**
  (scanned PDF, photograph, `.doc`, `.txt`) and did not retry. The supported
  formats are stated on the page before you upload.
- **You changed your mind after the report was delivered.** The result is
  delivered instantly and cannot be returned, so the sale is complete once the
  report is generated.
- **You no longer need the contract, or the client walked away.** That is outside
  our control and outside the Service.

If a scan fails, we would rather fix it than refund it: write to us first and we
will re-run it.

## 5. How to ask

Email {{email}} with:

- the email address or the Stripe receipt reference for the charge;
- the date and approximate amount;
- what went wrong (a screenshot of the error helps).

We reply within 5 business days. Approved refunds go back to the original payment
method through Stripe; how quickly it appears depends on your bank, typically 5–10
business days. We do not refund to a different card or account.

## 6. Your statutory rights

Nothing in this policy reduces a right you have under the consumer law that
applies to you.

If you are a consumer in the EU or the UK: digital content supplied immediately
is normally outside the 14-day right of withdrawal, but only where you gave
express consent to immediate delivery and acknowledged that you lose that right.
Where those conditions were not met, the withdrawal right applies and we will
honour it. If you are a consumer in a US state with its own refund rules, those
rules apply to the extent they give you more than this policy does.

## 7. Chargebacks

If you think a charge is wrong, write to {{email}} first — we can usually refund
it the same week. A chargeback started before you contact us takes much longer
for everyone, and we may have to suspend the account while the bank investigates.
