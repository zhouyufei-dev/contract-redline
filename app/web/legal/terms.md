## 2. What the Service does

1. You upload a contract as a text-based PDF or a DOCX file.
2. A deterministic rule engine — ordinary code, not a language model — checks the
   text against a fixed set of red-flag patterns and records the exact sentences
   that match.
3. Optionally, a third-party language model writes a plain-English explanation
   for each matched sentence. It is given the matched sentence and the rule that
   matched it, never your whole document, and it is not permitted to decide
   whether a clause is a risk.
4. You receive a report in your browser, which you can download as an HTML file.

**Scope.** The Service supports text-based PDF and DOCX files only. Scanned
documents and photographs of paper are not read and will produce an error rather
than a result. The rule library covers a fixed set of clause types common in
US-style freelance service contracts; it is not a complete review of a contract,
and a contract with no flags is not a safe contract.
